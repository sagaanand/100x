## The Problem Video Prospecting Solves

Cold email response rates have dropped 40% in the last two years. Volume has doubled. The inbox is full of AI-generated "personalized" messages that all sound the same.

Video breaks the pattern because:
1. A video thumbnail in an email stands out visually in a text-heavy inbox
2. Video conveys **tone, enthusiasm, and authentic personality** that text cannot
3. A short personalized video signals effort that is impossible to fake with automation

## The 10/30/10 Video Prospecting Formula

The core framework Morgan teaches to every sales team he works with.

### Structure
| Segment | Duration | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 10 seconds | Grab attention — reference something specific and personal to them |
| **Value** | 30 seconds | Explain why you're reaching out and what you can offer |
| **CTA** | 10 seconds | One clear, frictionless ask |

**Total video length:** 50 seconds (target), 35 seconds (advanced)

### The "Movie Trailer" Mental Model
> "Your videos should be like a movie trailer that hooks your prospect to want to learn more."

A movie trailer doesn't tell you the whole story. It creates enough interest that you want to see the movie. A prospecting video doesn't pitch your whole product — it creates enough relevance and curiosity that the prospect wants to respond.

## The Two Types of Prospecting Videos

### Type 1: Standalone Video (Webcam)
- **Format:** Direct to camera, 35–50 seconds
- **Best for:** Service-based selling, high-ACV deals, executive outreach
- **How to open:** "Hi [Name], the reason for my video is..."
- **Personalization:** Reference something specific they've said, posted, or announced

**Example opening:**
> "Hey Sarah — saw your post on LinkedIn about scaling your SDR team at [Company]. I work with a lot of VP Sales at Series B companies on exactly that transition. Wanted to share something specific that might be useful..."

### Type 2: Screenshare Video
- **Format:** Screen recording + picture-in-picture webcam, 45–60 seconds
- **Best for:** Product-led companies, technical buyers, warm outreach
- **What to show:** Their website, a recent update, a relevant metric or insight
- **Why it works:** Demonstrates you spent time researching specifically them

**Example use case:**  
Walk through their LinkedIn job postings, note they're hiring 5 SDRs, and explain specifically how your tool helps with SDR onboarding. The screen evidence proves your research.

## Six Technical Fundamentals

| Element | Why It Matters | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Eye contact** | Looking at camera = feeling of speaking to a real person | Look at the camera dot, not your face on screen |
| **Lighting** | Poor lighting = looks unprofessional | Face a window or use ring light |
| **Background** | Distracting backgrounds reduce watch time | Neutral background or virtual background |
| **Equipment** | Built-in webcam quality is often poor | Logitech C920 or better |
| **Posture** | Standing increases energy transfer | Stand at a standing desk or hold laptop at eye level |
| **Dress** | First impression in first 2 seconds | Match industry norms of your prospect |

## How to Open Every Video

Morgan's universal opener:
> "Hi [Name], the reason for my video is..."

**Why this opener:**
- Gets to the point immediately (no warm-up fluff)
- Signals intentionality — you have a reason, not just "checking in"
- The phrase "my video" reminds them this is a personal recording, not a broadcast

**What to insert after the opener:**
A compelling insight you discovered during research. Examples:
- "I noticed your team recently changed messaging on your homepage..."
- "I saw your post about [specific topic] and had a thought I wanted to share..."
- "Your latest product update suggests you're targeting [new segment]..."

## Call-to-Action Principles

Morgan's CTA philosophy: **never make the ask harder than the video warrants**.

| Video Quality | Appropriate CTA |
|---|---|
| Generic (just name + company) | "Worth a quick chat?" |
| Moderate (reference one thing) | "Would you be interested in learning more?" |
| High personalization (specific insight) | "I put together something specific for your team — would 15 minutes be worth it?" |

**What not to do:**
- Demand a 30-minute demo in a cold video
- Ask for a calendar link click in the first video
- Send a video with no CTA at all

## Scalability: Making Video Work at Volume

The misconception: video can't scale because you have to record each one.

Morgan's solution:
1. **Batch-record** personalized opening lines (10 sec) for 10–20 prospects
2. **Reuse** the value section (30 sec) for prospects in the same persona
3. **Add** a consistent outro (10 sec) for the CTA

This makes 50 videos in the time it takes most reps to write 50 cold emails — and converts at 10x the rate.
