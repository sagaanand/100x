## Post 1: Cold Calling Sucks — And That's Exactly Why It Works

**Source:** https://www.walnut.io/blog/video-series/nuts-about-sales-nick-cegelski-founder-of-30-minutes-to-presidents-club/  
**Also:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tzdqqNzG1cA

### The Core Argument
Cold calling is hard. Most reps avoid it. **That is precisely the opportunity.**

> "Cold calling sucks. That's why it works."

When everyone is sending automated cold emails and LinkedIn DMs, the bar for a good phone conversation drops to zero. The rep who actually calls — and who calls well — operates in a nearly uncrowded space.

### Why Reps Avoid Cold Calling
1. Immediate rejection (email rejection is delayed and invisible)
2. Performance anxiety (heard in real-time by a human)
3. No easy data to hide behind (email has open rates; calls have silence)

### The Reframe
Those same properties make cold calling:
- **Faster feedback loops** — you know immediately if your opener works
- **More human** — a real conversation is harder to ignore than an email
- **More memorable** — a good call creates a relationship that email can't

## Post 2: The Reply Method for Cold Email

**Source:** https://www.30mpc.com (Course: "The Reply Method for Double Digit Replies")

### The Framework (from 30MPC course outline)
Nick's cold email system is built around one goal: getting the prospect to **reply** (not book a call, not click a link — just reply).

Why? Because a reply is the first micro-commitment in a sales relationship. A prospect who replies once is 8x more likely to book a meeting than one who never responds.

### Key Principles
1. **Subject line: curiosity, not pitch** — never reveal your offer in the subject line
2. **First sentence: them, not you** — begin with an observation about them, not your company
3. **Body: short enough to fit on a phone screen** — if they need to scroll, they're gone
4. **CTA: single, easy, specific** — "Worth a 15-minute call?" beats "Would love to find time to connect"

### The Follow-Up Sequence
30MPC's "Perfect Outbound Sequence Template" (free resource):
- Day 1: Cold email (personalized)
- Day 3: LinkedIn connection request (with note)
- Day 5: Cold call
- Day 7: Cold email follow-up (different angle)
- Day 10: Cold call + voicemail
- Day 14: Break-up email ("Is this not a priority right now?")

## Post 3: 18 Cold Call Objections and Scripts

**Source:** https://www.30mpc.com (Free Resource)

### Category 1: Brush-off Objections
These aren't real objections — they're automatic responses from people who haven't engaged yet.

| Objection | Nick's Response |
|---|---|
| "Send me an email" | "Happy to — what specifically should I include to make it worth your time?" |
| "We're not interested" | "Totally fair — what made you say that?" (pause and listen) |
| "I'm busy" | "Completely understand. What's a better time — Monday morning or Tuesday afternoon?" |
| "We already have a solution" | "Good to know. What's working well about it?" |

The tactic: **respond with a question**, not a pitch. A question keeps the conversation alive.

### Category 2: Real Objections
These require actual answers:

| Objection | Response Strategy |
|---|---|
| "We don't have budget" | Explore timing: "When does your next budget cycle start?" |
| "The timing isn't right" | "What would need to change for the timing to be right?" |
| "We need to involve more people" | Multi-thread immediately: "Makes sense — who else should be on this call?" |

## Post 4: What Top 1% Reps Do Differently

**Source:** Backed by Gong data from 300M cold calls (30MPC methodology)

### The Cold Call Opening That Works
Based on analysis of millions of calls, the openers that work share these traits:
1. **State your name and company immediately** — don't hide who you are
2. **Give a reason for calling before asking anything** — "I'm calling because..."
3. **Ask a tied-back question** — something specific to their world that the reason implies

**Example:**
> "Hey [Name], this is Nick from [Company]. I'm calling because we worked with [similar company] and helped them [specific result]. I wanted to see if [specific challenge] is something you're dealing with at [their company]. Is that on your radar?"

### What the Data Shows
- Calls that open with "How are you doing?" convert **30% lower** than calls with immediate context
- Calls under **7 minutes** that advance to next step are more common than longer calls
- Voicemails that include a specific reason get **3x** more call-backs than generic ones

## Post 5: Multi-Threading and C-Suite Selling

**Source:** 30MPC Free Resource: "Multi-Threading and C-Suite Selling Report"

### Why Single-Threaded Deals Die
Most outbound starts with one contact. That contact:
- Gets promoted, changes jobs, or leaves
- Needs to get approval from someone you've never talked to
- Loses internal priority without a champion

### The Multi-Threading Approach
Start every outbound engagement with **3 contacts at minimum**:
1. The economic buyer (VP, CXO)
2. The champion (your daily user)
3. A stakeholder in adjacent function (IT, finance, or RevOps)

### How to Open the Top-Down Thread
Cold email to CXO:
> "Hi [CXO Name], I've been speaking with [Champion Name] about [problem]. I wanted to share a quick thought at your level — [one-sentence business case]. Worth 15 minutes?"

This creates accountability: the champion can't ignore you, and the CXO has context. Deals with executive engagement close at **2x the rate** of single-threaded opportunities.
