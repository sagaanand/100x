## Post 2: The 10/30/10 Video Prospecting Formula

**Sources:**  
- https://www.30mpc.com/newsletter/morgan-ingrams-10-30-10-video-prospecting-formula  
- https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/morgan-ingram-video-sales  
- https://ondemand.jbarrows.com/videos/how-to-build-a-video-prospecting-framework

### The Framework
Videos should be **under 60 seconds** and structured like a movie trailer:

| Segment | Duration | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 10 seconds | Grab attention — reference something specific to them |
| **Reason** | 30 seconds | Tell them why you're reaching out, what you see, what you're offering |
| **CTA** | 10 seconds | Single, clear ask — "Would it be worth a 15-minute call?" |

### Why Video Works
- Most LinkedIn inboxes are text-only — video thumbnails stand out visually
- Video conveys tone, enthusiasm, and authenticity that text can't
- A short personalized video signals effort > every automated email they receive

### Response Rate Data
Morgan achieves **45% response rates** with video outreach (vs. industry average of ~2–5% for cold email).

### How to Personalize Without Recording Every Video From Scratch
1. Record a generic intro (10 sec) once
2. Record a personalized middle section per prospect (30 sec)
3. Use a consistent outro (10 sec)

This makes video prospecting scalable without losing personalization.

## Post 3: LinkedIn Outreach Strategy for Executives

**Source:** Referenced in Leadfeeder B2B Rebellion series; Morgan J Ingram LinkedIn content

### The Problem with Generic LinkedIn DMs
Executives receive hundreds of connection requests and DMs per week. "Looking to connect with like-minded professionals" is immediately archived.

### Morgan's Approach: Research-First Outreach
1. **Comment genuinely** on their posts before DMing (2–3 comments over 1–2 weeks)
2. **Reference specific content** they've posted — quote one line back to them
3. **Lead with a relevant insight**, not a pitch
4. **DM only after they've seen your name** at least twice

### The Structure of a LinkedIn DM That Gets Replies
> "[Name], saw your post on [specific topic] — your point about [specific line] resonated.  
> Working with a few [job title]s on [related problem]. Would it be worth exploring?  
> Either way — [value give, like a relevant article]."

## Post 4: Fundamentals of Prospecting in 2025

**Source:** https://creators.spotify.com/pod/profile/1231798534/episodes/The-Secret-to-Successful-Outreach--with-Morgan-J-Ingram-e2rmils  
**Episode:** "The Secret to Successful Outreach" (Revenue Leaders podcast)

### Key Principles Morgan Teaches
1. **Volume does not equal pipeline** — more messages ≠ more meetings
2. **Personalization must be genuine** — referencing a name merge tag is not personalization
3. **Multi-channel is mandatory** — email + LinkedIn + video + phone, in coordinated sequence
4. **Timing matters** — reach out based on buying signals (job changes, funding, product launches), not calendar schedules

### The Signal-Based Trigger Morgan Uses Most
Monitor LinkedIn for when a prospect:
- Posts about a problem you solve
- Gets promoted into a decision-making role
- Announces a new initiative you can accelerate

This makes your outreach feel timely and relevant rather than random.

## Post 5: What He Teaches Sales Teams at Google, Salesforce, Snowflake

**Source:** https://www.hippovideo.io/blog/enterprise-sales-prospecting-morgan-j-ingram/

### Enterprise Prospecting Principles
1. **Multi-threading from day one** — connect with 3+ people at a target account before requesting a meeting
2. **Content as credibility** — share relevant articles/insights before asking for anything
3. **Video in email** — embed video thumbnails in cold emails to drive clicks vs. pure text
4. **Personalization at scale** — use Vidyard or Loom for batch-personalized video with custom first frames

### What Separates Good SDRs from Great Ones
> "The best SDRs I've coached don't make more calls. They make better calls, send better emails, and create experiences prospects want to respond to."

The differentiator: **effort signals**. A video, a custom line, a relevant article — these signal to the prospect that they specifically were chosen, not batch-added to a sequence.
