## Post 1: What AI Can't Do in Sales

**Source:** https://blog.jbarrows.com/blog/what-ai-cant-do-in-sales/  
**Published:** June 13, 2026

### The Core Argument
As AI automates email writing, prospect research, call planning, and sequence management — **empathy becomes the only durable competitive advantage**.

Machines can execute tactics. They cannot genuinely care about a customer's outcome.

### The GSF: Give a Shit Factor
John's term for authentic concern for solving a buyer's problem. The distinction he draws:
- Trainable skills (frameworks, objection handling, sequences) — AI can learn and replicate these
- Caring about the outcome — AI cannot feel the weight of what's at stake for the person you're talking to

> "AI cannot feel the weight of what's at stake for the person sitting across from you."

### The Research Supporting This
Center for Creative Leadership study of 6,700+ leaders across 38 countries: empathy correlates **directly with job performance** — measurably, not as a soft sentiment.

### Self-Assessment Questions by Role
- **Leadership:** Are your decisions influenced by human impact, not just metrics?
- **Managers:** Do you coach beyond quarterly numbers?
- **Individual Contributors:** Do you ask questions because you genuinely want to hear the answer?

### Implication for Cold Outreach
In a world where every sequence, subject line, and personalization can be AI-generated, the one thing that breaks through is a rep who actually gives a shit — who did real research, who asks real questions, who listens.

## Post 2: Restructuring a Sales Team for the AI Era

**Source:** https://blog.jbarrows.com/blog/restructuring-a-sales-team-for-ai/  
**Published:** June 12, 2026

### What Changes
- **SDR role** shifts from volume-based (dials/day) to signal-based (quality triggers)
- **Personalization** moves from "personalized at scale" to "genuinely researched"
- **Sequences** become AI-built but human-reviewed
- **Call prep** is largely automated — reps should spend 0 time on what AI can do faster

### What Doesn't Change
- Discovery quality — understanding buyer context is a human skill
- Relationship continuity — buyers still buy from people they trust
- Judgment calls — when to push, when to back off, when to loop in leadership

### The New Skill Stack for SDRs
1. Prompt engineering (getting AI to produce useful research)
2. Empathy and active listening (irreplaceable)
3. Storytelling (connecting data to human outcomes)
4. Signal reading (knowing which triggers matter)

## Post 3: The SaaSpocalypse

**Source:** https://blog.jbarrows.com/blog/future-of-saas-sales/  
**Published:** June 11, 2026

### What's Happening
The SaaS industry that built modern B2B sales is contracting. Consolidation, AI replacing point solutions, and buyer fatigue are reshaping the market.

### Implication for Outbound
- Categories that used to have 10 vendors now have 2–3 (consolidation creates both threats and opportunity)
- Buyers are more skeptical, better-informed, and more annoyed by outreach than ever
- The solution: outreach that treats buyers as intelligent adults with real problems, not leads in a funnel

### John's Thesis
The reps who will thrive in the next 3 years are those who can have a genuine peer-level business conversation — not those who can recite the best sequence formula.

## Post 4: Curiosity as the AI-Era Superpower

**Source:** https://blog.jbarrows.com/blog/curiosity-is-the-superpower-in-ai-sales/  
**Published:** June 6, 2026

### The Argument
AI makes all information available instantly. The differentiating human skill becomes: **knowing what questions to ask**.

> "Curiosity is the superpower in the age of AI."

### In Cold Outreach Context
- Don't open with what you know about the prospect — open with a question about what you don't know
- Discovery questions that demonstrate you've thought about their world create more trust than a pitch that shows you've researched their revenue
- The best cold calls are more diagnostic than persuasive

### Practical Application
Replace first-call pitching with first-call questioning:
- "What's driving your outbound initiative right now?"
- "What have you tried that hasn't worked?"
- "If we could solve one thing for you in the next 90 days, what would it be?"

## Post 5: Cold Email Framework (Pre-AI Era, Still Relevant)

**Source:** Referenced in multiple sources; from JBarrows training

### John's Cold Email Principles
1. **First ten words must be about them** — immediately signal this isn't a template
2. **Change subject line after 2–3 emails** — use "Still interested?" as a re-engagement trigger
3. **Value before ask** — don't request time before demonstrating relevance
4. **Short > long** — if you can't explain it in 5 sentences, you don't understand it

### The "Still Interested?" Sequence
After 2–3 unanswered emails:

**Email 4 subject:** Still Interested?

> "[Name], I've sent a few messages and haven't heard back. I don't want to keep emailing you if it's not relevant. If now's not a good time, just let me know and I'll reach out later. Either way, I won't unnecessarily email you again. — John"

This works because:
- It's honest and human
- It gives the prospect permission to say no (paradoxically increasing response)
- It demonstrates respect for their inbox
